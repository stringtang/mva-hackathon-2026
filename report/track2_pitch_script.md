## Production notes

- **Tone:** flat and factual. This is a real child; no dramatisation, no urgency music, no "race against time" framing.
- **No patient-identifying content.** No images, no name, no clinical photographs. Gene diagrams and protein structures only.
- **On-screen citations** as small footers where a specific result is claimed (Baker 2008; González-Blanco 2026; Presterud 2024; NCT03476330).
- **Slide 3 is the pivot** — hold it a beat longer than feels comfortable. Everything before sets it up; everything after follows from it.
- **Do not oversell slide 5.** The wording "would only pursue if we first saw" is deliberate and should not be tightened into a stronger claim.
