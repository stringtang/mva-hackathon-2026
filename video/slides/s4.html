Why we do not propose the expected answer

Clearing senescent cells is the famous result
in these mice. We are not proposing it.

It was genetic, never a drug

INK-ATTAC transgene plus a dimerizer with no clinical counterpart. No senolytic drug has ever been tested in a BubR1-insufficient model.

And it increased tumours

Removing p16 extended lifespan 25% — and produced significantly more tumours, 8 of 9 lung adenocarcinoma.

In a child who has already had a rhabdomyosarcoma,
that barrier is doing useful work.
Baker et al., Nature 2011 · Baker et al., Nat Cell Biol 2008
